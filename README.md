# EasyFind: GPS Navigation Extension

Directions to any place EasyFind can find: a town's trainer, a dungeon's entrance, a quest's objective, a spot on the map. It works out the whole trip from where you stand, flights, boats, zeppelins, the tram, portals, teleports and your hearthstone, then walks you the rest of the way.

## Requirements

- EasyFind

Retail, WoW Forever, Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and Mists Classic.

## Features

- Directions from where you stand, or a trip planned from any start.
- The steps in a small window, on the map's GPS page or in the objective tracker.
- An arrow at your character along the walk, the route on the map and the minimap, and a beacon where the current step leads.
- Trips with several stops, a search along the route, and routes shared as a link in chat.
- A red warning where the route crosses creatures far above your level.
- On WoW Forever, Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, walks follow the ground: around cliffs and water, down the ledges you can take, and around dangerous ground when you ask.
- A GPS button on EasyFind's search rows and map pins, and Directions on the objective tracker's and the quest log's menus.

## How to use it

Search for a place in EasyFind and click the GPS button on its row, or right-click a row or map pin and pick GPS. Open GPS from EasyFind's extensions menu to plan a trip from any start. Its settings are in EasyFind's options, under Extensions > GPS.
